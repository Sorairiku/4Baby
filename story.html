Sora and Hera

Our Story

A collection of the moments that brought us here.

01

Where it all started

We came from two different worlds.

She was a nursing student from FEU Manila, while I was studying Computer Engineering at FEU Tech. We weren't classmates, we weren't from the same branch, and honestly, there wasn't really any reason for our paths to cross.

But somehow, they did.

We met through a mutual friend, JB. Looking back, it's funny to think that someone who eventually became such a small part of our lives was the reason the two of us ever started talking.

When I first saw her, I thought she was cute.

The funny thing was, I already had a crush on someone else at the time. And apparently, she wasn't even trying to get together with me. She initially wanted to set me up with one of her friends.

So naturally, I did what any reasonable person would do.

I messaged her first.

I thought she was cute, I had a feeling she might like me, and I figured...

why not?

That little message turned into conversations that lasted through the day and into the night. One conversation became another, and somewhere along the way, we started liking each other more and more.

I eventually found out that one of the reasons she initially liked me was because she thought I was rich and had a car.

Honestly, looking back, that's still one of the funniest things about how we started.

But whatever her reason was at the beginning, it became something much bigger than that.

02

The first month

One of the memories I will never forget happened during our first month of talking.

I had just gotten home from La Union, and instead of simply going home and resting, I went to see her.

I wanted to surprise her.

We walked around and talked for what felt like forever, together with two of her childhood best friends. Eventually, we ended up eating at McDonald's.

It was such a simple day.

Nothing extravagant.

Just walking, talking, eating, and spending time together.

But before I left, something happened that I would remember for years.

Our first kiss.

Or, more accurately...

I kind of stole a kiss from her. Hahaha.

And somehow, from that little moment, everything started changing.

03

Falling

At the beginning, I think she was the one who fell harder.

But somewhere along the way, things completely flipped.

I became absolutely head over heels for this girl.

The more I got to know her, the more I loved her. What started with a random message became days and nights of talking, learning about each other, laughing together, and slowly becoming a part of each other's lives.

Eventually, she wasn't just someone I talked to.

She became my person.

We studied together.

We traveled together.

We spent countless nights talking nonstop.

We shared things about our lives that we probably wouldn't have shared with anyone else.

And somewhere in all of those ordinary moments, I realized that I had never loved someone the way I loved her.

04

We were never perfect

Our story wasn't perfect.

We fought.

A lot.

Especially because I could be easily irritated, and I know there were many times when I wasn't the easiest person to be with.

But despite all of that, she stayed.

She saw the good parts of me, the bad parts of me, and everything in between.

And I stayed too.

We had our difficult moments, including a time when it felt like we might actually lose each other. We came close to falling apart around March 2024, but somehow, we found our way through it.

We weren't a perfect couple.

We didn't have perfect communication.

We didn't always agree.

We had different interests, different personalities, and sometimes completely different ways of looking at things.

But we always found our way back to each other.

For years, that was our story.

Two imperfect people trying to make something work.

05

The years we shared

Looking back, I think some of the best parts weren't the big moments.

They were the ordinary ones.

The nights where we talked until we didn't realize how late it had gotten.

Studying together.

Eating together.

Traveling together.

Laughing at stupid things.

Getting annoyed at each other.

Making up.

Finding something new to talk about even after we'd already spent the entire day talking.

It became normal to have her in my life.

And I think that's what makes this so difficult now.

Because she wasn't just part of my life.

She became part of the life I imagined for myself.

When I thought about the future, I naturally pictured her there.

06

And then there was us

Somewhere between that first message, that first walk, that stolen kiss, all the arguments, the travels, the late-night conversations, the studying, the laughter, and all the ordinary days in between...

Years passed.

And I realized that the girl I once messaged simply because I thought she was cute had become the person I loved more than anyone.

I don't know if every story is meant to have the ending we imagine when we're in the middle of it.

Maybe ours isn't finished.

Maybe it is.

I don't know.

But I do know this:

For the years that our lives found each other, I wouldn't trade them for anything.

I loved you.

I love you.

And whatever happens from here, you will always be one of the most important chapters of my life.

My last message to you
